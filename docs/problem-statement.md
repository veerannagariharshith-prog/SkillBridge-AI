# Problem Statement

## Mapping Skill Gaps to Industry Needs

Students often have difficulty understanding whether their current skills match the requirements of industry and available job roles.

The proposed SkillBridge AI platform helps identify existing skills, compare them with industry requirements, identify missing skills, and provide suitable career and skill-development recommendations.

## Objective

The objective is to develop a platform that helps users:

- Identify their existing skills
- Understand industry-required skills
- Detect skill gaps
- Find suitable job roles
- Identify skills that need improvement
- Improve their career readiness

## Proposed Solution

SkillBridge AI provides a centralized platform for skill analysis and industry matching.

The system analyzes user skills and compares them with job requirements. It then displays matched skills, missing skills, and potential job matches.

This approach is intended to help students make better decisions about their skill development and career planning.
